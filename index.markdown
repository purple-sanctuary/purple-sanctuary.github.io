Purple Sanctuary is a group of Northwestern University students, grad workers, faculty, and staff focused on protecting our campus from ICE.

We created [a petition](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zGj2_BSQ3qMkEO9cZdLj9209QARVhSSeD5DWHFEFi58/edit) to encourage the NU administration to create clear and effective ICE policies. If you are in the NU community, including alumni and parents of students, please read and consider signing.

We'd love to expand our group. To join our organization, or to get in touch in general,
please email us at [purple-sanctuary@proton.me](mailto:purple-sanctuary@proton.me).
You can also follow our Instagram account, [purple.sanctuary.nu](https://www.instagram.com/purple.sanctuary.nu/).

## More info

We are encouraging the NU administration
to create clear and effective ICE policies,
in line with the policies of our peer institutions.
We have sent letters to President Chiang, Vice President Graham,
and Vice President Figora laying out the need for ICE policies in line with the policies of other similar universities.
We created [our petition](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zGj2_BSQ3qMkEO9cZdLj9209QARVhSSeD5DWHFEFi58/edit)
after hearing no response.
Over 300 community members have signed so far.

If you're excited to do more, please [get in touch](mailto:purple-sanctuary@proton.me)! We also have [a flyer](/assets/nu-ice-petition.pdf), which we'd love to have posted on bulletin boards.

We are working to organize Know Your Rights trainings to aid community members
in understanding our rights when ICE enters our community or enters our campus.
