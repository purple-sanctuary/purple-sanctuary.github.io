Purple Sanctuary is a group of Northwestern University students, grad workers, faculty, and staff focused on protecting our campus from ICE.
To that end, we are encouraging the NU administration
to create clear and effective ICE policies,
in line with the policies of our peer institutions.
We have sent letters to President Chiang, Vice President Graham,
and Vice President Figora laying out the need for such policies and requesting to meet.
Having heard no response, we have created [a petition](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zGj2_BSQ3qMkEO9cZdLj9209QARVhSSeD5DWHFEFi58/edit)
laying out the need for such policies,
which we invite members of the NU community,
including alumni and parents of students,
to read and consider signing.
Over 250 community members have signed so far.
We also have [a flyer](/assets/nu-ice-petition.pdf),
which you can post on bulletin boards to get the word out further.

We are working to organize Know Your Rights trainings to aid community members
in understanding our rights when ICE enters our community or enters our campus.
