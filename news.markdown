### Death of Morton Grove resident just after ICE deportation {#death-deportation}

[‘Heartbreaking and entirely preventable’: Chicago-area man died after deportation, family says](https://chicago.suntimes.com/immigration/2026/11/10/chicago-area-man-dies-after-deportation-to-pakistan-family-attorney-says), Chicago Sun-Times, November 11, 2026.

Ayaz Ahmed, a Morton Grove resident, was arrested by ICE in June 2026.
Morton Grove is a nearby suburb east of Evanston.
According to his family, while he was in custody ICE denied him his vital meds,
put him in isolation as punishment for asking for his meds,
and his health worsened severely.
Despite his poor health,
he was deported to Pakistan,
his country of origin,
around August 1, 2026.
His health worsened further on the flight,
according to other people on the flight.
According to his family,
he collapsed on the tarmac after landing, and died within the next 3 days.

### Evanston resident hospitalized after attempted arrest by ICE {#hospitalized}

* [US citizen injured after ICE agents reportedly mistook him for fugitive](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/24/citizen-injured-ice-mistaken-fugitive-arrest), the Guardian, September 24, 2026.
* [Evanston man, a U.S. citizen, injured in Sunday confrontation with U.S. immigration officers](https://chicago.suntimes.com/immigration/2026/09/21/evanston-us-citizen-injured-federal-immigration-agents), Chicago Sun-Times, September 21, 2026.
* [Evanston resident mistakenly detained by ICE Sunday morning, EPD says](https://evanstonroundtable.com/2026/09/20/ice-wrongly-detains-evanston-resident/), Evanston RoundTable, September 20, 2026.

Just before 10am on Sunday, September 20th, a Black Evanston resident and US citizen was walking to a pharmacy to pick up a prescription. In front of [El Mexicano Pueblito Grill](https://www.elpueblito2.com/) in South Evanston, ICE tried to arrest him. He shouted "I'm a citizen, leave me alone.", according to an eyewitness, as they wrestled him to the ground and put a knee on his back, pinning him down. His face showed injuries: his right eye socket and temple were swollen, perhaps from being punched, and his face was scraped, perhaps against the ground, according to an eyewitness. ICE didn't stop until a small crowd of bystanders stood nearby, yelling at them.

Ryan Garton, who filmed the incident, urged the ICE agent to get off the Evanston resident so Ryan could inspect his injuries. The ICE agent now relented, and stepped away. His shirt bore hand-shaped bloodstains. The ICE agents got in their cars and sped away, leaving him bleeding on the ground. ICE later claimed they left the area after protesters began to gather because they feared they would be attacked.

Michael Lipson, the owner of a store across the street, corroborated Ryan's account and the [video evidence](https://www.facebook.com/share/v/18GnQy4HC9/?mibextid=wwXIfr). Michael laid out his sweatshirt for the Evanston resident to rest on while Ryan looked over his injuries. He was taken by ambulance to [St. Francis Hospital](https://saintfrancisevanston.org/) in Evanston with injuries to his head, neck, and teeth.

Evanston Police have been investigating, and have [shared their own body camera evidence with the press](https://evanstonroundtable.com/2026/10/06/federal-agent-who-mistakenly-detained-injured-u-s-citizen-identified-in-epd-records/). Using the footage, the press was able to identify one of the ICE agents involved as Esteban Rivas. Later in the body camera footage, a paramedic says "so ICE just tackled the guy and took off?". An EPD officer responds "Yeah they didn't even bother to … I know I'm actually in shock that somebody would … I'm at a loss for words right now."

In response to this incident, Evanston Mayor and US Representative-elect [Daniel Biss called to](https://x.com/DanielBiss/status/2102857894890807365) "Abolish ICE, hold the Trump administration accountable, and end the funding of these brutal invasions into our cities."

### ICE vehicle collision and violence, Halloween 2025 {#halloween}

* [One year after Halloween incident, fear remains over immigration enforcement](https://evanstonroundtable.com/2026/10/18/one-year-after-halloween-incident-fear-remains-over-immigration-enforcement/), October 18th, 2026, Evanston RoundTable.
* [Evanston stands strong amidst ICE presence](https://www.evanstonian.net/feature/2025/11/21/evanston-stands-strong-amidst-ice-presence/), November 21, 2025, The Evanstonian.
* [In Chicago, a Halloween weekend of immigration arrests and violence](https://www.npr.org/2025/11/02/nx-s1-5594866/chicago-evanston-ice-border-patrol-protest-immigration-illinois), November 2, 2025, WBEZ Chicago.
* [Federal agents arrest 3 US citizens after crash during immigration operation in Evanston](https://abc7chicago.com/post/ice-evanston-today-news-cook-county-officials-speak-federal-immigration-agents-reportedly-area-halloween/18095995/), November 1, 2025, ABC7 Chicago.

On October 31, 2025, Halloween, Evanston was on alert due to ICE agents in the area. Their first arrest occurred around 9:30am. The Evanston school districts closed their campuses to outside visitors that day. Around noon, five vehicles of ICE agents were driving through Evanston, and Evanston community members were following the car to warn people of their presence. The ICE vehicles were driving erratically, running stop signs and red lights. At the intersection of Asbury and Oakton, witnesses saw the agents slam on the breaks just past the intersection, causing Chicago resident Nancy Polanco, who was not part of the group following the agents, to crash into them. This intersection is a block from both [Chute Middle School](https://chute.district65.net/) and [Oakton Elementary School](https://oakton.district65.net/). Neighbors say the agents then violently extracted Nancy from her car and handcuffed her.

When neighbors came to see what happened, ICE agents pulled out guns and pepper spray. As a crowd formed, ICE agents pepper sprayed several residents. Evanston Fire Department paramedics responded to provide medical care for those exposed to pepper spray.

ICE agents violent arrested two bystanders, Chicago resident Marcello Africano and Evanston resident Jennifer Moriarty. ICE agent Thomas Parsons repeated punched Marcello in the head while pinning him to the ground, and dragged his face across the pavement after he was handcuffed, as [filmed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V1BkPl_3Wic) from [multiple angles](https://bsky.app/profile/royalpratt.bsky.social/post/3m4ji6clac22n). In one video, the man tells the agents that he is unable to breathe.

Jennifer got out of her car to film, and said that she had no warning to step back before an ICE agent "turned around and just dropped me", knocking off one of her shoes. He grabbed her by the neck and tossed her to the ground.

Matthew, an Evanston resident, filmed ICE agent Richard Donahue violently arresting a woman. Matthew recalled that he told Donahue, "You got to stop that", to which Donahue responded "Step back or I'll [expletive] shoot you". Matthew stepped back, and then Donahue turned around and pointed a gun at him, as seen on video.

Jennifer, Macello, and Nancy were all detained in the same car. All three are US Citizens. Jennifer said that Donahue drove all over Evanston and Rogers Park for several hours. She said that Donahue repeatedly slammed on the breaks to try to get another car to hit his vehicle. She also said that he got out of his car and ran to the car behind with his mace out on three occasions, at which point one of the other ICE agents told him to stop. She also said that Doanhue threatened three times to Mace her and the other detainees in the car. Marcello said he told ICE that he needed medical help in the first hour after the crash, but received no aid for several hours. The three detainees were released after several hours from the Chicago FBI headquarters.

Marcello was charged by ICE with assaulting, resisting, and impeding ICE, which was dismissed in February. Nancy and Jennifer were not charged.

ICE claims that they arrested 5 undocumented immigrants from Mexico on Halloween. None were accused of any violent crimes.

Evanston Policy Chief Schenita Stewart is in communication with the Cook County State's Attorney's office on a potential prosecution. Jennifer Moriarty is a member of a group of 15 Illinoisans who have [moved to sue DHS](https://evanstonroundtable.com/2026/09/20/evanston-resident-jennifer-moriarty-ice-complaint/) under the Federal Tort Claims Act for damages over these violent actions. She has also given public comment at City Council meetings and testified before the Illinois Accountability Commission, which called for potential prosecution of ICE agents Timothy Donahue, Thomas Parsons, and Edward Maxa for their actions in this incident.
